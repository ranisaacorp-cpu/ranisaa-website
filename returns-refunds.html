RETURNS & REFUNDS

Returns & Refunds Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

We want customers to receive their Ranisaa products in good condition. Our final return, refund and cancellation terms will be applied according to the order and the checkout terms shown at the time of purchase.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If an item arrives damaged or you receive an incorrect item, please contact contact@ranisaa.co.in as soon as possible with your order number and clear photographs of the package and product.

Return Requests

Return eligibility may depend on the product type, condition and applicable shipping requirements. Products that have been opened, used or altered may not be eligible for return where applicable.

Refunds

If a refund is approved, the refund method and timing will depend on the payment method and payment provider used for the order. Approved refunds will be processed after the applicable return or order review is completed.

Shipping Costs

Any shipping charges associated with a return or refund will be handled according to the specific circumstances of the order and the checkout terms displayed at purchase.

Contact Us

For a return, refund or cancellation question, email contact@ranisaa.co.in with your order details.

Note: This policy should be reviewed and finalized against Ranisaa's actual shipping, cancellation and return procedures before live checkout is activated.
