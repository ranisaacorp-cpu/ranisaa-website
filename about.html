ABOUT RANISAA

Sacred traditions, thoughtfully presented.

Ranisaa is a dedicated pooja and spiritual products brand inspired by Indian traditions and devotional practices.

Our collection is being developed around products and ritual collections intended for prayer, pooja and meaningful spiritual occasions.

Our Collections

  • Ranisaa Premium Dhoop Cones and fragrance collections
  • Ganga Jal
  • Navgrah Pooja articles and collections
  • Grah Shanti Pooja articles and collections
  • Additional pooja essentials introduced over time

Our Approach

We aim to present traditional products in a simple, respectful and modern way for customers in India and around the world.
