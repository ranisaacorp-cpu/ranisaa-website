Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Ranisaa respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website or contact us.

Information You Provide

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, order information and the contents of your message.

Website Information

Our website may receive basic technical information through hosting and security systems, such as browser, device and network information, for normal website operation and security.

Shopping Cart

The current website cart is used to prepare a purchase and does not itself collect or process your card or bank details. When secure online checkout is activated, payment and order information may be processed by the payment provider used by Ranisaa. This policy will be updated with the relevant provider information before live checkout is launched.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to customer enquiries
  • To provide customer and order support
  • To operate and secure the website
  • To process purchases once online checkout is enabled

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the website, process payments, fulfill orders, provide shipping, or maintain security.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@ranisaa.co.in.
